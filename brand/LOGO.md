# The OpenPay mark

One idea carries the whole identity: **the signal ring with a gap, and the dot in the gap**. The ring is the network; the gap is the dead zone; the dot is the payment that gets through anyway. Nothing else needs explaining.

## Construction

- A circle, stroke only, with a 40 degree gap centered at the top right (315 degrees)
- A solid dot sitting in the gap, on the ring path
- Stroke is round capped, weight 16% of the canvas
- Dot radius is 11% of the canvas

The canonical geometry lives in three places that must stay visually identical:

- `site/icons/*.png` (PWA and favicon, generated by `site/icons/make-icons.py`)
- `android/.../res/drawable/ic_launcher_foreground.xml` (the launcher icon)
- inline SVG in `site/index.html`, `site/verify.html`, `site/app.html` (the `MarkIcon` composable is the Android twin)

## Variants

| Use | Form |
|---|---|
| Default | green `#0E7C46` on paper `#FAFAF7` |
| On dark | bright green `#2FBF71` on ink `#0D0F0E` |
| Monochrome | single color, any of the palette, no dot fill change |
| App icon | mark at 36% radius on paper, maskable variant at 27% with safe margin |

## Wordmark

`OpenPay`, set in Space Grotesk 700, sentence case, letter spacing -0.01em. Never all caps, never spaced out, never with a tagline baked in.

## Wrong uses

- Rotating the mark so the gap points elsewhere
- Adding anything inside the ring
- Outlines around the dot, gradients on the ring
- The word "Pay" in a different weight than "Open"

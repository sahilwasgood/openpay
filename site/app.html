Balance *99*3#
First time with *99#?

Dial *99# once. Reply with your bank's short name, for example SBIN for SBI or HDFC for HDFC, or the first four letters of your IFSC. Choose the account to link, then set your UPI PIN with your debit card details when asked. That one setup unlocks every *99# payment after it. Your PIN lives with the carrier menu, never on this page.

Choose a rail

Your PIN is typed in the UPI app, on the carrier menu, or on the call keypad. This page never sees it.

A Note is a promissory note, a digital khata slip: signed by this browser's key, checked offline, honoured over UPI later. Max Rs 2,000 each, Rs 10,000 open.

Open notes

Merchant mode. Scan a customer's Note, or paste the text. Signature, expiry, the Rs 2,000 cap and replay by serial are checked on this device.

Received

Browsers cannot read your bank SMS, so nothing here confirms on its own. Mark payments paid when your bank's SMS arrives. Everything stays in this browser.

OpenPay is not a bank and not a PSP. Payments settle on NPCI's UPI rails through your bank. Works offline once installed.

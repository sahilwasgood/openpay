Verify a Note in the browser.

No Android app needed. Paste the Note text the payer sent you, and this page checks the signature, the expiry, the amount cap and the fields, all on your device. Install this site as an app once and it keeps working with zero signal.

This browser cannot check Ed25519 signatures. Use Chrome or Edge 113+, Safari 17+, or Firefox 129+, or the Android app.

A Note is a promissory note, a digital khata slip: a signed "I owe you" that settles over normal UPI later. No money moves here. Verification happens on your device, this page sends nothing anywhere.

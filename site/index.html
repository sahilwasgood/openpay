The new fee

The Rs 2,000 line, explained.

From Oct 15, 2026, UPI charges large merchants 0.4% on person to merchant payments above Rs 2,000, capped at Rs 300. The customer pays nothing, ever. Person to person transfers stay free. Small merchants are exempt.

  • Will splitting Rs 10,000 into 5-6 parts save tax? No. MDR is not tax. Income tax looks at total receipts, not transaction size, and hiding turnover is evasion, not planning.
  • Does splitting save the payer money? No. The payer pays zero MDR either way. The fee comes out of the merchant's proceeds.
  • Does splitting even work? No. NPCI prohibits splitting one purchase into smaller payments and applies the MDR to the total value received for a single invoice. It monitors for it.

Genuine personal transfers stay free. For recurring payments, the legitimate automation rail is UPI Autopay mandates.

FAQ

Questions people actually ask.

Does it work on Jio?

Yes. Jio's all IP network carries no USSD, so OpenPay moves to the UPI 123Pay phone line and enters the details with dial tones. Same money, same rails, no menus.

What if the payee does not have the OpenPay app?

With any signal at all, the USSD and IVR rails pay any UPI user, no app needed on their side. With zero signal, they can verify a Note in any browser: open the verify page, paste the note text. Install the web app once and it keeps verifying offline.

Does it track my spending?

Optionally, and only on your phone. With your permission the app reads your own bank debit SMS and builds a private monthly spending log. There is no internet permission, so that data physically cannot leave the device. Off by default it is not: it is on, clearly labelled, and one switch turns it off.

Can I split a bill?

Yes. The Split tab cuts a total into fair shares down to the paisa and signs one Note per person for the same UPI ID. Show each QR, and they verify offline like any Note.

What if the payer never settles a Note?

A Note is a signed, timestamped promise for a small amount. The pending tracker counts down NPCI's clocks (T+1 auto reversal, T+5 refund deadline) and writes the escalation for you. The signature is your evidence.

Is OpenPay a wallet?

No. It never holds money and never touches your funds. Online, your bank app pays. Offline, a Note is a promise that settles through your bank app later.

What does it cost?

The app is free and open source. Person to person UPI is free. Shops already pay MDR above ₹2,000 like any UPI payment. OpenPay takes nothing.

When can I install it?

The Android APK is on Releases, and this site installs as an app too: tap Install web app above. v0.3 adds Notes, merchant verify, Hindi, elder mode, and CSV export.

Why not iOS?

Apple does not let an app dial USSD or IVR sessions. A typing companion for iPhone is planned. The full ladder needs Android.

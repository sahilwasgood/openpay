FAQ

Questions people actually ask.

Is there an APK?

No. OpenPay is a PWA. Open app.html in your browser, tap Install or Add to Home Screen, and it works like an app. There is nothing to sideload and no store permission to fight.

Does it work on Jio?

Jio's all IP network carries no USSD, so OpenPay opens the UPI 123Pay phone line and enters the details with dial tones. For a data pack, use the recharge handoff: the 123Pay voice menu or your operator's own app.

Can I buy a data pack with no data and no balance?

Yes, if you have money in your bank. SBI Quick and Axis Bank both accept an SMS recharge that debits your account and credits the operator. OpenPay writes the message for you. You send it from your own phone.

Who gets the money?

The operator. In the bank SMS and UPI 123Pay paths your bank debits you and the operator is credited. OpenPay is never the payee and never holds a balance.

Does the web app see my bank SMS?

No. Browsers cannot read SMS, so you mark a payment confirmed when your bank message arrives. Nothing is sent anywhere to check it.

Is OpenPay a wallet?

No. It never holds money. Your bank app pays online, and the recharge handoff only writes the instruction you send yourself.

Does it work offline?

After the first load, yes. The interface, your history and the recharge instructions are cached in your browser. The handoff still needs a mobile signal because it uses SMS, USSD or a voice call, not mobile data.
